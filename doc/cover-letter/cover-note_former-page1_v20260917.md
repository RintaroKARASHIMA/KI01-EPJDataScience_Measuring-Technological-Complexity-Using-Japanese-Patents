Dear Editor:

We are grateful for your constructive comments and the valuable suggestions provided by the reviewers. We appreciate this opportunity to resubmit our manuscript, titled “Measuring Technological Complexity Using Japanese Patents,” for publication in EPJ Data Science as an original research article. The manuscript ID is c2970727-07a5-4193-93d3-809ed46060d2.

In response to the feedback, we have thoroughly revised our paper, incorporating all comments and suggestions from the reviewers. Both reviewers raised serious concerns about whether our analysis supported the paper’s primary claims. We carried out the additional analyses the reviewers requested, and in two respects the results confirmed the reviewers’ concerns rather than merely qualifying them. A subsampling test shows that most of the apparent advantage of the corporate-level measure over the regional-level measure reflects the number of actor nodes rather than the corporate level itself, and the original prefecture-to-prefecture comparison could not be defended against a placebo null distribution, so we have withdrawn that analysis and its figure. In their place, we added a new out-of-sample validation in which corporate-level TCI predicts subsequent regional technological entry while regional TCI does not. During the revision we also found and corrected several errors in the submitted manuscript that the reviewers had not flagged but that affected passages they commented on, and these are disclosed in the individual responses below. We report all of this directly in the point-by-point response below, together with the corresponding revisions to the Abstract, Introduction, Results, Discussion, and Methods. Manuscript locations cited in the responses refer to the revised manuscript, and page and line numbers refer to its line-numbered PDF.

In accordance with the submission requirements, we have prepared the following files:

1) A “Point-by-point response” to the reviewers' comments

2) The “Manuscript file”, submitted as a LaTeX source archive with all figures included, for compilation by the production system

3) A “Related file”: the revised manuscript with tracked changes relative to the submitted version, for the reviewers' reference

We believe we have addressed all concerns raised and hope that our paper is now suitable for publication. We look forward to your feedback.
